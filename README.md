## FAQ

### What is XCutors?

XCutors is a curated collection of popular Roblox executor tools bundled together with a universal loader. It provides a convenient way to access and manage multiple script executors from a single package.

### Which executors are included in XCutors?

The package includes four popular executors: Volt, Synapse Z, Potassium, and Wave. It also includes xcutorsLoader, a universal loader that lets you manage and switch between all included executors.

### Is XCutors free to use?

Yes, the XCutors loader and the collection package are free to download from this repository. However, some individual executors may have their own licensing terms or premium tiers.

### What are the system requirements?

XCutors requires Windows 10 or later, a 64-bit processor, at least 4 GB of RAM, and an active internet connection for updates and script hub access.

### Why do I need to run the loader as Administrator?

Administrator privileges are required for the injection process to attach to the Roblox client process. Without elevated permissions, the executor cannot communicate with the game.

### Can I use my own custom Lua scripts?

Absolutely. All included executors support custom Lua scripts. You can paste scripts directly into the editor, load them from local files, or browse the built-in script hub.

### How do I update the executors?

XCutors includes a built-in auto-update system. When you launch xcutorsLoader, it automatically checks for and applies any available updates to keep all executors compatible with the latest Roblox patches.

### Which executor should I choose?

Each executor has its own strengths. Volt is known for speed, Synapse Z for stability and broad script compatibility, Potassium for its lightweight design, and Wave for its modern interface. Try each one to see which suits your needs best.

### My anticomponent flagged the loader. Is it safe?

Some anticomponent programs may flag script executors due to the nature of the injection process. We recommend adding the XCutors folder to your anticomponent exclusion list. Always download only from this official repository.

### Where can I find scripts to use?

XCutors includes a built-in script hub with a curated selection of popular community scripts. You can also find scripts on popular Roblox scripting forums and communities.
